Move AI Coach

A coach inside the world

The MOVE AI COACH lives inside Worlds in Motion. It teaches the way a great coach teaches: show the movement, explain it plainly, watch the attempt, and adjust what comes next.

  • Explains technique in plain language, one cue at a time, so a five year old and an adult athlete both get it.
  • Demonstrates movement in front of you, so you can follow along and mirror what you see.
  • Adapts difficulty to your level. Courses and challenges scale as your skill grows.
  • Encourages with honesty. Credit for real progress, clear guidance when something needs work.
  • Tracks progress across every world, so your record shows how far you have come.
Honest limits. The coach recognises only what headset sensors can reliably verify, and it teaches movement only. It gives no medical diagnosis and no medical advice.

MoveVerse Academy

Built for real PE

MOVEVERSE Academy brings the worlds into physical education. Lessons are aligned to standards and built for real class schedules, with the teacher in control from the first minute.

  • Standards aligned lessons drawn from the worlds, ready to run in a normal class period.
  • Teacher dashboard with class progress at a glance, designed with working PE teachers in mind.
  • Student progress records that follow each participant across sessions and worlds.
  • Printable reports for the records schools actually keep.
Safety first, on paper and in product. Academy privacy controls are part of the design from the start. Accounts for children arrive only after child safety and legal review is complete.

MoveVerse Arena

Play together, later

Arena is the shared world of MOVEVERSE: safe, moderated multiplayer where movers meet, team up and challenge each other. It is a later phase of the platform. Arena opens only after child safety and legal review is complete, and no date is promised. When it opens, it opens safe.

One Account
