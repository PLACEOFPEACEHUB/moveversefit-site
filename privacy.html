Privacy Policy

Starter policy for a product in development. Last updated November 27, 2026.

Who we are

MOVEVERSE is the World of Movement, a VR movement platform now in development. This website is its official home. This policy covers this website only. The game, MOVEVERSE Academy and MOVEVERSE Arena are in development, and their privacy practices will be published before any of them launch.

What this site collects

This site collects only what you choose to send through the early access form:

  • Signup type (player, beta tester, teacher or school, or parent)
  • First and last name
  • Email address
  • School or organization, only if you choose to add it
  • A message, only if you choose to write one

Form submissions are processed and stored by the website host that serves this site. Browsing this site does not create an account, and the site itself sets no cookies and runs no advertising trackers. The fonts on this site load from Google Fonts, and Google may receive your IP address as part of serving those font files.

How we use it

  • To write to you when an early access wave opens for your signup type
  • To plan beta testing, school programs and family launch timing
  • To answer a question you sent us in the message field

We do not sell personal information. We do not share it with advertisers. We share it only with the service providers who run this website and our email, and only so they can do that work.

Children

The early access form is for adults: players aged 18 and over, parents, teachers and school staff. Accounts for children are a future phase of the platform and arrive only after child safety and legal review is complete. If you believe a child sent personal information through this form, contact us through the routes on this site and we will delete it.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to show you the information you sent, correct it, or delete it and remove you from the early access list. Contact us through Instagram at @MoveVerseFit or through the form on this site, and we will act on your request.

Changes

MOVEVERSE is in development, and this policy will grow with the product. When it changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top.
